# 0012 The logo is the "cr" mark of portal.confref.org

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-08 · Supersedes [0008](0008-logo.md)

## Context

[0008](0008-logo.md) chose the "cr" mark; variant 1 of the eight early variants (`old/confref-logos.svg`) was to be
redrawn for the website. The mark that portal.confref.org actually used from 2019 (web archive, `logo-home.png`) is a
later, refined design of the same idea (made by Net Wise's designer in January 2019, after Kai Eckert's preference for
variant 8): a three-quarter ring in teal and an r in red, whose stem runs from the top into the ring.

## Decision

- **The logo is the mark of portal.confref.org**, redrawn as SVG from its PNG
  (`confref-site/static/img/confref-mark.svg`: no masks, so it works on dark grounds), with the colours teal
  `#66ccce` and red `#f03554`.
- The wordmark is set in the site's typeface: "conf" in the text colour, "ref" in red.
