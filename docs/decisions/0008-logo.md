# 0008 The "cr" mark is the logo of confref

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-11-08

## Context

Two logos exist (`old/` in the workspace): the "cr" mark (eight vector variants, `old/confref-logos.svg`), designed by
Net Wise's designer in January 2019 under the contract funded by the Digital Science Catalyst Grant; and the
"conference portal" logo, sent by Net Wise on 2018-10-04, probably the logo of the earlier Springer conference portal.

## Decision

- **The "cr" mark is the confref logo**, with its colours teal `#66ccce` and red `#f03554`, and a neutral grey.
- The "conference portal" logo is not used.
